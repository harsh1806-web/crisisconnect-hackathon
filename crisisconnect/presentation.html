The Critical Problem

The 3 Fatal Communication Failures in Every Disaster

In catastrophes like the Chennai floods or Hurricane Helene, rescue resources exist, but coordination fails. Information breakdown costs lives.

Breakdown 01

Helpline Congestion

Traditional 112/911 phone lines face 95% call congestion within 15 minutes. Panicked callers get busy tones and struggle to explain exact coordinates over voice.

➔ Result: Hours of delay
Breakdown 02

Social Media Chaos

WhatsApp and Twitter are inundated with stale rumors and duplicate pleas. 50 relatives post for the same flat, causing rescue teams to visit the same house 5 times.

➔ Result: 70% wasted dispatches
Breakdown 03

Total 4G/5G Blackout

High-speed cellular packet towers and power grids submerge. Standard emergency portals freeze and crash, leaving victims trapped in digital dark zones.

➔ Result: Complete silence
