The Real-World Problem

The 3 Fatal Communication Failures in Every Catastrophe

When cyclones, floods, or major disasters hit, rescue resources exist, but coordination collapses. Communication failure is what claims lives.

Failure 01

112 Helpline Congestion

Traditional emergency switchboards face 95% busy-tone congestion within 15 minutes. Panicked callers cannot explain exact coordinates over voice in darkness.

➔ Hours of rescue delays
Failure 02

Social Media Duplication

Twitter and WhatsApp are flooded with stale pleas. 50 neighbors post about the same apartment, sending rescue boats to the same street 5 times while others drown.

➔ 70% wasted boat dispatches
Failure 03

Total 4G/5G Blackout

Cellular data packet trunks submerge or lose generator power. Standard government websites freeze and crash, leaving victims in complete digital dark zones.

➔ Victims completely abandoned
